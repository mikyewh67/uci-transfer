Strategy: TAG & backups

UCI Fall 2028 TAG: UCI currently excludes both Business Administration and Business Economics from TAG for Fall 2028. Do not build the plan around a UCI guarantee. Keep UCI Business Administration as the direct-business reach and UCI Business Economics as a related but still selective option.

Real guarantee lane: use the UC Transfer Admission Planner and check UCR's Fall 2028 TAG rules when that cycle publishes. UCR Business Administration is a strong business-school backup, but the app labels it “annual verification required” until the official 2028 major list is confirmed.

Application spread: UCI Business Administration, UCLA Business Economics, Berkeley Haas, UCR Business Administration, USC Marshall, plus at least one additional UC/CSU safety. Your SMMZolo work belongs in activities and PIQs with measurable results, not exaggerated claims.

Glossary — the jargon, decoded

ASSIST
The official CA database showing exactly which SMC courses count at which UC, course-for-course. The source of truth for your plan.
Articulation
"This SMC course = that UCI course." If it's not articulated on ASSIST, assume it doesn't count.
Cal-GETC
The CCC general-education pattern available to new students beginning Fall 2025. It can clear most lower-division GE at many campuses, but it is not required for UC admission and does not replace major prep.
TAG
Transfer Admission Guarantee — an official guarantee only for participating campus/major/year combinations. UCI excludes Business Administration and Business Economics for Fall 2028.
TAU
Transfer Academic Update — after applying, you report fall grades and planned spring courses. Submit a complete update by the published priority deadline.
PIQ
Personal Insight Questions — the UC "essays." Short answers, 350 words, transfers do 4.
Merage
The Paul Merage School of Business at UC Irvine. Business Administration transfer admission is selective and is reviewed within UCI's transfer process.
Corsair Connect
SMC's student portal — enrollment appointments, registration, records. Your enrollment date posts here each term; earlier = better classes.
