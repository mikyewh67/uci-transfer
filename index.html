Strategy: TAG & backups

The TAG question: Business Admin has no TAG, but Business Economics at UCI does (needs 3.4 GPA + econ and calc with B or better — verify current-year rules). Catch: your TAG major must match your primary major on the UC app, so TAG'ing Business Econ means applying to that at UCI, not Business Admin. One or the other at UCI — decide with a counselor by Sep 2027.

Spread the risk: One UC app covers all campuses — adding more is cheap. Consider UCLA (Business Econ), UCR/UCSB (Econ), and a CSU app (CSULB/CSUF business are strong + ADT-friendly). Fee waivers cover up to 4 UC campuses if eligible.

Your angle: a transfer applicant running two real businesses with revenue is rare. Grades get you past the filter; the story wins the review.

Glossary — the jargon, decoded

ASSIST
The official CA database showing exactly which SMC courses count at which UC, course-for-course. The source of truth for your plan.
Articulation
"This SMC course = that UCI course." If it's not articulated on ASSIST, assume it doesn't count.
Cal-GETC
The general-ed package (replaced IGETC in Fall 2025). Complete it and GE is settled at every UC.
TAG
Transfer Admission Guarantee — guaranteed UC admission if you hit set numbers. Business Admin doesn't offer it; Business Econ does.
TAU
Transfer Academic Update — in January you log your fall grades + spring classes into your submitted UC app. Miss it and your app dies quietly.
PIQ
Personal Insight Questions — the UC "essays." Short answers, 350 words, transfers do 4.
Merage
The Paul Merage School of Business — UCI's business school, runs its own transfer advising on top of UC admissions.
Corsair Connect
SMC's student portal — enrollment appointments, registration, records. Your enrollment date posts here each term; earlier = better classes.
